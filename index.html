Halloween is a famous holiday that takes place on October 31 of every year. It dates back to over 2,000 years ago as an ancient festival in Ireland and northern France. Marking the end of the harvest and the start of winter, Halloween celebrates the living and the dead. Many people wear costumes to scare away spirits, and children always look forward to going Trick or Treating from door to door to get candy.
